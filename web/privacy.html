Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bridge Software (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Dead Dispatch (the “Game”). By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information that personally identifies you (such as your name, email address, or phone number). The Game does, however, rely on the third-party services listed below, which may automatically collect limited technical information from your device.

1.1 Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, and preferences (for example, current level, coins, audio settings) are stored locally on your device using standard OS storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data from your device.

1.2 Crash and diagnostic data

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive automated crash reports so we can diagnose and fix issues. These reports may include:

  • Device model, operating system version, and language;
  • A pseudonymous installation identifier;
  • The state of the Game at the time of the crash (stack trace, the screen you were on, non-personal logs);
  • Approximate region inferred from your IP address (the IP itself is not stored).

We use this data solely to improve stability and quality of the Game. See Google’s Firebase Privacy Information for more details.

1.3 Advertising

The Game shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where you have permitted its use);
  • IP address, general location, device type, and operating system;
  • Interaction with ads (impressions, clicks).

This information is used to deliver, measure, and (where applicable) personalize ads. You can review Google’s practices in the Google Advertising Privacy Notice and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game;
  • To diagnose crashes, performance problems, and bugs;
  • To serve and measure advertisements that help fund development;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Your choices

3.1 Advertising identifier

You can limit or reset your advertising identifier at any time:

  • Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and toggle off Allow Apps to Request to Track.

3.2 EU / UK / Swiss users

Where required by law, the Game presents a consent dialog (Google’s User Messaging Platform) the first time you launch it. You can change your choice at any time from the Game’s Settings screen.

3.3 Local game data

You can clear all locally stored Game data by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in your device settings.

4. Data sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • With the service providers listed above (Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob), which act as processors for the limited data described in this Policy;
  • Where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

5. Data retention

Crash diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Advertising data is retained by Google in accordance with its own retention policies. We do not maintain our own copy of this data beyond aggregated, non-identifying reports.

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. International users

The Game is operated from outside the European Economic Area. By using the Game, you understand that the limited technical information described above may be processed by Google and its sub-processors in countries where they operate, including the United States, subject to appropriate safeguards.

8. Security

We rely on the security practices of our third-party providers for any data transmitted off your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Effective date” above. Material changes will be highlighted in the Game or on this page.
